# PRS

Polygenic risk scores (PRS) are statistical models that estimate an individual's propensity for trait of interest from his or her genotype.


## Construction of PRS

As is standard in statistical learning, PRS are constructed via a fitting procedure. We use training data consisting of the genotypes and trait values of individuals from a population of interest to build a model relating genotype to trait value.  This model than then be used to predict trait values from genotypes for previously-unseen individuals.



## Applications of PRS

The core clinical application of PRS is in risk stratification. For example, an individual with a very high PRS score for heart disease might be given a preventive treatment regime even when completely asymptomatic.
